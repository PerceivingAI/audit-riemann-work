## 5. Comparative Mathematical Normalization Insights

1. **Laguerre Representation**:
   * The structural link between the classical Li test polynomial and $L_{n-1}^{(1)}$ is standard in Lagarias (2007): $P_n(x) = L_{n-1}^{(1)}(-x)$.
   * The candidate claim in `riemann-conjecture` (`CLM-LAGU-001`, `CLM-LAGU-002`) isolates the explicit deterministic zeta-pole exponential mode $1 - q^n$ with $q = -s_0/(s_0-1)$ and builds the exact shift-filter $T = (E-1)(E-q)$ to annihilate it.
2. **Schoenberg & Conditionally Negative Definite Formulation**:
   * Prior literature (Gröchenig 2020) connects RH to Schoenberg **total positivity** of kernel transforms.
   * Prior literature has not formulated the sequence $\psi(n) = \lambda_{|n|}$ directly as a conditionally negative definite function on $\mathbb{Z}$ generating the Schoenberg convolution semigroup $e^{-t\lambda_{|n|}}$. This confirms `CLM-GRAM-002` addresses an unmapped structural formulation in literature.
